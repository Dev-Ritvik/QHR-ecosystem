@@ type-scale-sprawl | SYSTEMIC | systemic | body-two-sizes, numerals-mixed
L: All pages at 1920×945 (measured on 28 pages and the film).
E: The type system: sizes, line heights, letter spacing.
O: One typeface is used well, but its settings have multiplied. There are 15 text sizes on the inner pages (10.24, 10.75, 12.8, 16, 16.8, 20, 23.1, 25, 31.25, 31.67, 44.1, 46.62, 63, 90.3, 121.8 px) and two more on the film's footer (13.44 and 134.4 px); 42 distinct combinations of size, weight, spacing and leading; 13 letter-spacing values; six different line heights at 20 px (27, 29, 30, 31, 32, 32.4) and eight at 16 px. The small capitals come in two sizes a half-pixel apart: 10.75 px in the header and 10.24 px on every other control.
V: Computed styles of every text element on every page.
W: The eye cannot name a 0.5 px or a 1 px difference in leading, but it reads the sum as "assembled". An authored system has seven or eight sizes and one leading per size.
X: A scale of about eight sizes, one line height for each, one small-capital size.
